     mentoring.

  3. NEET Long Term Coaching (Repeaters & Droppers) — in-depth NCERT

  4. EAMCET Long Term Coaching — one focused year for a better EAMCET

TEACHING / ACADEMIC SYSTEM
Rankridge combines teaching, practice, testing, analysis and mentoring in
one structured model:
- Expert (IITian & experienced) faculty guidance.
- Daily practice and subject-wise assignments.
- Regular tests and mock exams with performance analysis.
- Error analysis and correction (finding where and why marks were lost).
- Doubt clarification support.
- Personal mentoring for confidence and discipline.
- Parent progress updates (attendance, tests, improvement areas).
- Rank and college prediction counselling.
- Hostel & day scholar options (based on availability).
- Scholarship support for deserving, eligible students.
Academic philosophy: Teach clearly, practice daily, test regularly,
analyse deeply, correct quickly, mentor personally, communicate honestly.
